Public Timeline

Entrance Nightclub Incident

The team attends the data drop at Entrance Nightclub. Pretty Boy acquires the Chinese chessboard device containing the hidden data chip.

A rival runner team triggers a blackout across several blocks. Violence erupts on the third floor.

Eileen McTavish, personal aide and close friend of the Mayor of Seattle, is killed by a Mana Ball during the fighting.

Aftermath

The team escapes, receives warning from their fixer, has their SINs scrubbed, and is sent into hiding. Red's vehicle is destroyed as part of damage control.
