Entrance Nightclub Data Drop

Objective

Intercept a concealed data chip during a covert exchange at Entrance Nightclub.

Outcome

Success

The data chip was recovered and the team escaped.

Reward

100,000¥

Operational Deployment

  • Cloak accessed the roof and guarded rooftop entry.
  • Pretty Boy bought VIP access to the third floor and acquired the target.
  • Red stayed on the second floor as muscle.
  • Arjun waited near Tanuki Mart for interception or support.

Incident Summary

Pretty Boy recovered a Chinese chessboard-style device containing the hidden data chip. A rival shadowrunner team entered the scene at roughly the same time, with one runner using a RACS stealth suit to bypass Cloak on the roof.

The rival team's hacker cut power across several blocks. In the chaos, the insect mage attacked the Yakuza, Hammer opened fire, and a Mana Ball killed Eileen McTavish.

The team's fixer warned them not to deliver immediately. Their SINs were scrubbed, Red's vehicle was destroyed, and the runners were sent into hiding.
