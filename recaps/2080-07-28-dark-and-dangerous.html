Recovered session recap

Dark and Dangerous

A warning, a bargain, five missing hours and the impossible pattern inside Mr. K.

28 July 2080 // 4 of 4 parts recovered

The session began by rewinding the timeline to cover Pretty Boy’s off-screen actions from the previous session.

Pretty Boy travelled to Arjun’s dog sanctuary after Arjun requested the help of a fellow mage. Arjun had been experiencing increasingly strange supernatural phenomena whenever he cast spells or interacted with the astral. Unknown to either character, these disturbances were the result of Mr. Dark’s growing influence over Arjun.

At Arjun’s request, Pretty Boy assensed him. With an exceptionally good result, he detected that something was subtly wrong with Arjun’s aura. The corruption was incredibly faint, appearing as a thin film over his astral signature. Pretty Boy concluded that Arjun should seek an exorcism or examination from a highly skilled professional mage, perhaps even a corporate magical specialist. They parted ways, Arjun locked up the sanctuary and went to bed.

As Pretty Boy walked away through the evening streets, Mr. Dark’s gum toads and crawlers manifested around him. Pretty Boy recognised the danger and attempted to leave. Mr. Dark casually manifested against a nearby wall and began a deliberately cryptic conversation, warning that Pretty Boy’s friends were in mortal danger and that time was running out.

Pretty Boy refused every attempt to bargain, making it clear he knew Mr. Dark was deceptive. As he walked away, he dropped a Fireball behind him, vaporising the gum toads. The crawlers, however, were astrally present rather than physically manifested, allowing the spell to pass harmlessly through them. They continued following him.

Mr. Dark appeared again farther down the street and eventually forced a choice: allow one of the crawlers to bite him, or willingly bite one of the gum toads. Through persistent resistance and negotiation, Pretty Boy reduced what would have been a far more dangerous pact into something comparatively limited. Mr. Dark would be able to locate Pretty Boy once at some point in the future, and Pretty Boy would owe him one further conversation. Nothing beyond that was explicitly agreed.

After the bargain was sealed, reality shifted. Pretty Boy suddenly found himself sitting in the passenger seat beside Pyrite, who was escaping after being shot by gangers. Neither character had ever met before, making Mr. Dark’s warning about “saving your friends” deeply unsettling. Pretty Boy attempted to heal Pyrite while Pyrite drove them clear. Only afterwards did Pretty Boy realise approximately five hours had disappeared.

Meanwhile, the gunshot that wounded Pyrite caused Mr. K to panic while travelling through the Barrens with Red. Mr. K attempted to flee. Red easily outran him, physically stopped him and calmed him before escorting him toward the safehouse. Cloak remained largely in the background after securing it.

Once everyone had regrouped, they allowed Mr. K to finish sleeping before questioning him further. While he rested, Pretty Boy used Assensing to examine him. The astral signature he observed was unlike anything he had encountered.

Within Mr. K’s aura he saw tiny blue pinpricks moving throughout the brain and spinal cord, suggestive of countless microscopic constructs moving through the nervous system. The aura around the brain had been profoundly altered. Instead of appearing like a normal metahuman mind, portions resembled streams of Matrix code embedded directly into his astral pattern.

Although Pretty Boy lacked the terminology, the examination revealed that Mr. K was both a Technomancer and a Monad. He also detected extraordinarily sophisticated augmentations integrated almost seamlessly into Mr. K’s aura, implying access to delta-grade and highly experimental technology worth an estimated ¥3–5 million.

The party began to understand why Mr. K was terrified of cameras, SIN scanners and modern technology. They theorised that his altered brain might involuntarily establish Matrix handshakes with nearby wireless devices simply by existing, making technological detection a constant risk.

Armed with that understanding, the team revised its extraction strategy for the upcoming yacht party. Pyrite called in a favour with Grox, arranging hire of the party boat for ¥3,000 and approximately twenty escorts, low-level socialites and party guests as convincing background cover for ¥2,000. The session ended inside the safehouse, ready to depart for the party boat.
