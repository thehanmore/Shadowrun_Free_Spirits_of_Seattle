Recovered session recap

“I Thought We Had a Deal”

The party boat extraction succeeds. Everything after that gets complicated.

04 August 2080 // 8 of 8 parts recovered

The Party Boat

Red, Cloak, Pretty Boy and Pyrite continued escorting Mr. K to his arranged extraction point. Pretty Boy called in a favour from a trusted lieutenant in an ork gang. The lieutenant arrived in a gigantic red-and-chrome muscle car, accompanied by a heavily cybered and armed gang member, to provide additional muscle.

They moved from the safehouse toward the district near the northern bridge connecting to the Renraku Arcology. The team successfully boarded the party boat arranged for the next stage. Their rent-a-crowd boarded with them, and everyone’s disguises held up—including Mr. K’s.

Mr. K laid false Matrix trails reporting that he had been sighted in the northern Redmond Barrens. The deception worked: Renraku security teams diverted north while the real Mr. K headed south by river.

The Northern Bridge

Renraku had turned the bridge into an enormous checkpoint. Dozens, possibly hundreds, of troops were deployed alongside armoured vehicles, scanning equipment and an Armoured Warfare Unit—a small mech equipped with a rotary assault cannon. The party boat passed beneath without incident.

Once safely beyond it, Mr. K asked Pretty Boy how he would feel if the Armoured Warfare Unit were hacked to create a substantial distraction. Pretty Boy eventually gave him the green light, provided nothing could blow back on the team.

As both a technomancer and a monad, Mr. K could reach systems remotely, including jumping air gaps that should have isolated a device. He broke into the Armoured Warfare Unit and turned its rotary assault cannon on the Renraku forces around it.

The bridge descended into chaos. The hijacked unit opened fire, security resources poured toward the bridge, and DocWagon was called after one of three senior security executives overseeing the operation was specifically targeted and killed. Meanwhile, the party boat continued quietly south.

Mr. Dark Makes an Offer

Toward the end of the river journey, Mr. Dark manifested beside Mr. K, leaning casually against the railing. Pretty Boy overheard him address Mr. K by another name: Hiroshi Takamoto.

Mr. Dark said he could get his body back—not another body, but his original body reconstituted. There was a price, and Mr. K apparently already knew it. He refused.

Pretty Boy interjected that Mr. Dark was probably telling the truth. Mr. K had finally had enough. He snatched a champagne bottle from the cooler and swung it at Mr. Dark’s head. The bottle passed through empty air. Mr. K staggered and caught the table. Mr. Dark was gone.

Harbour View

The team disembarked near Harbour View Medical Center. Pretty Boy stayed aboard longer and travelled about a kilometre farther south. There he spotted a rigger operating a drone in short, sharp search circuits around buildings, underpasses and car parks.

Pretty Boy cast an illusion over a nearby vehicle, making it appear that something strange had happened to it. The rigger focused on the bait, allowing Pretty Boy to slip away and rejoin the others.

The Gym

The team moved to a twenty-four-hour gym they had scouted as somewhere to wait out the remaining twelve to fifteen hours before delivery. They had privately booked a sauna upstairs. Cloak remained outside as scout while Red, Pyrite, Pretty Boy and Mr. K stayed inside.

Mr. K wanted snacks. Pyrite volunteered to get them, despite having been warned about two gym bros who thought they recognised her. The dwarf and human approached her near the snack machine. The dwarf pulled out his phone and tried to record Pyrite and get photographs with her. Pyrite attacked him.

A scuffle broke out. The dwarf tried to flee while live-streaming Pyrite. His friend intervened, but Pyrite chased the dwarf down the hallway and launched a flying kick into his back. She warned the team they had been made. Red emerged to investigate while Pretty Boy moved Mr. K rapidly toward the exit.

Red convinced Pyrite to stop beating the dwarf, then took the dwarf’s phone and crushed it. The pair fled, but the dwarf struck the fire-alarm button on his way out. The alarm sounded and the sprinklers activated.

Mr. K hacked the building’s power and shut it down. The team piled into its recently hijacked vehicle. With security services responding, Mr. K hacked the local grid and blacked out the entire block. Under cover of darkness, the team disappeared.

Underground

They entered the tunnel system Cloak had scouted. The journey was uneventful. They descended beneath the functioning subway station to an older, disused station and an antiquated security room full of CRT monitors. Mr. K had arranged to meet his extraction contact there.

Before leaving, Pretty Boy insisted on their payday. Mr. K hesitated, then transferred the agreed funds. The runners started back toward the functioning station.

Behind them, they heard Mr. K’s raised, panicked voice: “I thought we had a deal!”

The team debated going back, but decided they had delivered him and been paid. Then Mr. Dark whispered directly into Pretty Boy’s ear: “I really think you’ll find it interesting. You should go have a look.” The team turned around.

The Security Room

Pyrite entered first and found Mr. K’s decapitated body. His head was gone. More disturbingly, his heart was missing, but there was no entry or exit wound explaining its removal. Every nanite that had permeated his central nervous system was also gone.

The runners found another credstick containing 20,000 credits, in addition to the separate 20,000-credit credstick given to Pretty Boy earlier. Tucked into Mr. K’s breast pocket was one final object: a folded Joker.

With Mr. K—or Hiroshi Takamoto—lying headless in the abandoned security room, his heart inexplicably removed and every trace of his nanites gone, the session ended.
