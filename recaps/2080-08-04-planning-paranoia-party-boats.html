Recovered session recap

Planning, Paranoia and Party Boats

Four reconnaissance reports converge into a river extraction plan.

04 August 2080 // 4 of 4 parts recovered

The session began with Cloak and Red remaining at the Barrens safehouse to guard Mr. K, while Cloak conducted the initial reconnaissance.

Cloak scouted the party boat departure location and identified a nearby, relatively secure fallback position. During his reconnaissance he observed a heavy Renraku security presence on the bridge opposite the departure point, on the arcology side of the river. Among the security assets was a large mechanised combat platform armed with what appeared to be a rotary assault cannon, making a direct bridge crossing extremely dangerous.

Pyrite conducted separate reconnaissance farther south toward the intended disembarkation point. He investigated a private dock and nearby buildings where the team intended to leave the party boat by swimming the final stretch to shore. Nearby, Pyrite located a twenty-four-hour gym, which the team planned to use as a temporary safe location after leaving the boat and before making the final leg to the mall.

Pyrite also noted surveillance drones patrolling the river approximately once per hour, although their owner could not be determined. A light but regular flow of small boats and pleasure craft provided civilian traffic that might assist with concealment.

Separately, Cloak identified a tunnel near the mall that appeared to offer a relatively safe underground approach for the final kilometre of the mission.

Later, Cloak established an observation position atop a tall building in the north-central Barrens and monitored three separate teams believed to be Renraku security personnel, all actively sweeping the Barrens for Mr. K and the runners.

Mr. K explained that he had penetrated the Renraku Arcology’s security systems and created a false Matrix trail placing him in the northern Barrens as a deliberate distraction. He intercepted Renraku communications and learned that security forces believed they had located him, only for the lead to prove to be the team’s previous safehouse. The deception had bought valuable time.

The session concluded with the team finalising the infiltration plan. Pretty Boy called in a favour from a valuable contact, arranging for an ork associate with a heavily modified muscle car to transport the team from the Barrens to the safe location near the party boat departure point. From there, they intended to begin the river phase of the operation.
