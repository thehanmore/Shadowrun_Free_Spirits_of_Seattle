Recovered session recap

Extraction Planning

A compromised hideout, inexplicable alterations and a diversion that nearly killed Pyrite.

21 July 2080 // 2 of 2 fragments recovered

With Pretty Boy absent, the rest of the team travelled to Mr. K’s temporary hideout to discuss the extraction job in detail. During the meeting, Mr. K made it clear just how dangerous the situation had become, stressing that multiple megacorporations were likely searching for him and that anyone helping him would be putting themselves at considerable risk. His growing paranoia and frayed mental state were becoming increasingly difficult to ignore.

To ensure Mr. K wasn’t carrying hidden trackers or surveillance devices, the team called in Dr. McCoy, Cloak’s trusted street doc contact, to examine him using equipment from his medical van. The scan found no detectable bugs or implants, but it did reveal unusual genetic alterations that Dr. McCoy admitted were well beyond his expertise. Whatever had been done to Mr. K, it wasn’t something a street clinic could explain. The discovery only deepened the mystery surrounding the job.

While the examination was taking place, a Halloweeners van slowly cruised past the apartment. The gang members spent an uncomfortable amount of time watching the building and appeared particularly interested in the medical van, but ultimately continued on without taking hostile action. Whether they had recognised someone, suspected something or were simply curious remained unclear.

After Pyrite spotted a corporate scout or rider on an expensive bike coasting nearby, and believing the current safehouse might already be compromised, Cloak set out across the rooftops to locate a more secure location. Pyrite accompanied him. After carefully scouting the surrounding area, Cloak found an abandoned storefront that could be entered and secured without leaving obvious signs of forced entry.

Meanwhile, Pyrite decided to create a diversion to draw attention away from Mr. K’s location. After acquiring a run-down vehicle, he drove it into a nearby gang-controlled parking area, sideswiping a row of parked motorcycles and throwing the area into chaos. The diversion succeeded in pulling attention away from the safehouse, but not without cost. One of the gang lookouts fired a well-placed shot from a Remington 950, punching through the vehicle and seriously wounding Pyrite before he escaped.

As Red began escorting Mr. K through the back alleys toward Cloak’s newly secured hideout, the distant rifle shot proved to be the final straw for the already fragile executive. Overcome by panic, Mr. K broke away and fled into the alleys of the Barrens, forcing Red to give chase.

The session ended with Mr. K fleeing through the Barrens, Red in pursuit, Cloak waiting at the newly secured hideout, and Pyrite wounded but having successfully drawn attention away from the team’s true objective.
