Session recap // 11 August 2080

“Thumbs up.”

RECONSTRUCTED FROM ORDERED DISCORD PARTS

Part 1/8 // The Security Room

The runners resumed beneath Southgate around Mr. K’s decapitated body. Pyrite saw her own face on a security monitor and, unable to find the storage system, destroyed monitors, cables and racks. Prettyboy turned instead to the folded Joker and examined its astral signature in detail.

Part 2/8 // Cloak Explores the Tunnels

Cloak spent several minutes checking abandoned subway tunnels and service spaces. He located a heavily fortified old steel door fitted with a modern high-end biometric reader, and a separate ladder leading to a street-level manhole behind the station.

Part 3/8 // Debrief at Cheap Trix

At Cheap Trix, a Renraku satellite-company employee offered a possible explanation for the bridge deployment: an assassination attempt against a Renraku security chief. DocWagon arrived to recover its client; Renraku refused to release him because nobody knew who could be trusted.

Part 4/8 // The Bridge Aftermath

The standoff turned lethal after a Renraku soldier raised a rifle toward DocWagon personnel. DocWagon responded immediately, killed numerous Renraku soldiers and recovered its contracted client.

Part 5/8 // Grox Calls the Team

Grox summoned the runners to the Circuit Breaker—not for a job, but for a conversation. He asked whether Prettyboy truly believed Mr. K was dead and raised the possibility that a technomancer might continue in the Matrix beyond the body.

Part 6/8 // Five Minutes to Discuss Mr. Dark

Grox activated a magical protection fetish and a technological silencing field. The protection would last five minutes. He warned Prettyboy not to say another dangerous name aloud and explained that Mr. Dark should not be understood as an ordinary enemy.

Part 7/8 // The Warning

Mr. Dark was described as manipulative, Machiavellian and capable of arranging people and events as pieces in a game whose objective might never become visible. Conventional assumptions about money, revenge, ideology or victory may not apply.

Part 8/8 // End of the Night & Viral Video

The night ended with drinks and separate plans. By then, footage of Pyrite and Red had gone viral. Friends of Humanity amplified it past one million views, distributing their faces while constructing its own narrative around the incident.
